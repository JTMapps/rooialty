TABLE: cart_items

COLUMNS:
- cart_id (uuid)
- created_at (timestamp with time zone)
- id (uuid)
- item_id (uuid)
- quantity (integer)

CONSTRAINTS:
- FOREIGN KEY (cart_id) REFERENCES carts(id) ON DELETE CASCADE
- FOREIGN KEY (item_id) REFERENCES items(id)
- PRIMARY KEY (id)
- CHECK (quantity > 0)

RELATIONSHIPS:
- FOREIGN KEY (cart_id) REFERENCES carts(id) ON DELETE CASCADE
- FOREIGN KEY (item_id) REFERENCES items(id)

BUSINESS RULES:
- CHECK (quantity > 0)
