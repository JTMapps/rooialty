TABLE: items

COLUMNS:
- category (item_categories (URBAN KOTAS | ROOIALTY MEALS | TO SHARE | WINGS | WING BAR | COLD SERVES))
- deleted_at (timestamp with time zone)
- id (uuid)
- in_stock (boolean)
- item_type (item_type (food | drink))
- name (text)
- price (numeric)

CONSTRAINTS:
- PRIMARY KEY (id)
- CHECK (price >= 0::numeric)

BUSINESS RULES:
- CHECK (price >= 0::numeric)
