TABLE: profiles

COLUMNS:
- created_at (timestamp with time zone)
- email (text)
- id (uuid)
- is_active (boolean)
- last_seen_at (timestamp with time zone)
- phone (text)
- role (user_role (user | clerk | office))
- username (text)

CONSTRAINTS:
- UNIQUE (email)
- FOREIGN KEY (id) REFERENCES auth.users(id) ON DELETE CASCADE
- PRIMARY KEY (id)
- UNIQUE (username)

RELATIONSHIPS:
- FOREIGN KEY (id) REFERENCES auth.users(id) ON DELETE CASCADE
