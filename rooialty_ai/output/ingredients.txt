TABLE: ingredients

COLUMNS:
- cost_per_unit (numeric(10,4))
- created_at (timestamp with time zone)
- deleted_at (timestamp with time zone)
- description (text)
- id (uuid)
- name (text)
- reorder_level (numeric(10,3))
- reorder_quantity (numeric(10,3))
- supplier_note (text)
- unit (unit_of_measure (g | kg | ml | l | unit | portion))
- updated_at (timestamp with time zone)

CONSTRAINTS:
- PRIMARY KEY (id)
- CHECK (reorder_level >= 0::numeric)
- CHECK (reorder_quantity > 0::numeric)

BUSINESS RULES:
- CHECK (reorder_level >= 0::numeric)
- CHECK (reorder_quantity > 0::numeric)
